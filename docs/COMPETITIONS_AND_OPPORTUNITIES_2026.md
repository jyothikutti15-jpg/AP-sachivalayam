## KEY DIFFERENTIATORS TO HIGHLIGHT IN APPLICATIONS

1. **Scale:** 1.3 lakh (130,000) village secretariat employees across Andhra Pradesh
2. **Language inclusion:** Telugu voice input - serving non-English government workers
3. **Channel:** WhatsApp-based - meeting users where they already are (no new app to install)
4. **AI backbone:** Claude AI (Anthropic) - responsible AI for government
5. **Four critical features:** Scheme eligibility, form automation, grievance tracking, burnout prevention
6. **Rural impact:** Secretariat employees serve India's last-mile citizens in villages
7. **Burnout prevention:** Novel employee wellness angle for government workers
8. **Sovereign AI angle:** Localized Telugu language model for Indian government context
9. **Cost efficiency:** Reduces per-application processing time, directly measurable ROI
10. **Replicability:** Model can be replicated across all 28 Indian states and 8 UTs
