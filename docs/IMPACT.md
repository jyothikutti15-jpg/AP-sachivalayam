## 4. Citizen Impact

### Repeat Visit Reduction

**Problem:** Citizens make 2-3 visits to the secretariat per scheme application because they don't bring the right documents or miss deadlines.

**With AI Copilot:**

| Intervention | Visits Saved |
|---|---|
| Document Checklist (Feature 8) | 1st visit covers all documents for all schemes |
| Citizen Reminders (Feature 10) | No missed deadlines — auto WhatsApp reminders |
| Outreach Scanner (Feature 6) | Citizens notified proactively — no need to ask |
| Form Auto-Fill (Feature 2) | Fewer errors = fewer rejections = fewer re-applications |

**Estimated reduction: 40-60% fewer repeat visits**

### Scale

```
4.9 crore citizens
x ~30% interact with secretariat annually (1.47 crore)
x average 3 visits per interaction
= 4.41 crore citizen-visits/year

40% reduction = 1.76 crore visits saved/year
```

### Citizen Time Saved
```
Average round-trip to secretariat: 2-4 hours (rural AP)
1.76 crore visits x 3 hours = 5.28 crore citizen-hours saved/year
```

### Citizen Cost Saved
```
Average travel cost: Rs 50-100 per visit (auto/bus fare)
Average lost wages: Rs 200-400 per half-day
1.76 crore visits x Rs 250 average cost = Rs 440 crore citizen savings/year
```

## Methodology Notes

1. **Employee count:** 1,30,000 is based on AP government's stated staffing for 11,162 secretariats (average 11-12 staff per secretariat across 10+ designations: VRO, Welfare Assistant, ANM, etc.)

2. **Salary data:** Average Rs 25,000/month is based on AP government pay scales for Group-IV employees (VROs: Rs 18,000-22,000; WEAs: Rs 20,000-25,000; Digital Assistants: Rs 15,000-20,000)

3. **Working days:** 275 days/year (365 minus weekends and gazetted holidays per AP government calendar)

4. **Citizen data:** 4.9 crore is AP's population per Census 2011 projections for 2026

5. **Scheme data:** All eligibility criteria and benefit amounts sourced from official portals (thallikivandanam.com, sspensions.ap.gov.in, annadathasukhibhava.ap.gov.in)

6. **Grievance data:** AP Spandana portal (spandana.ap.gov.in) public statistics

7. **Efficiency factor (55%):** Applied to gross salary savings to account for adoption curve, non-monetizable time, and overhead. Based on similar GovTech deployments in India (DigiLocker, UMANG app adoption studies).

---

> These are projections based on published data and reasonable assumptions. Actual impact should be measured through a controlled pilot deployment across 2-3 districts with before/after metrics tracking.
