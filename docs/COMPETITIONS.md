## Pitch Narrative

Use this as a starting template for competition applications. Adapt tone and length per competition requirements.

---

### The Problem

Andhra Pradesh runs the world's largest last-mile governance system — 11,162 village and ward secretariats employing 1.3 lakh staff who serve 4.9 crore citizens. Each employee manages 34 government departments, processes applications for 30+ welfare schemes, handles citizen grievances, and generates daily reports.

They do all of this from memory.

An employee answering a citizen's question about pension eligibility must recall income limits, age criteria, ration card requirements, and exclusion rules — across 12 pension subcategories. A wrong answer means a citizen loses benefits they're entitled to. A missed grievance deadline means an SLA breach that goes unreported. A forgotten document means the citizen makes another 20-kilometer trip to the secretariat.

The People's Pulse survey (January 2026) found that decision overload across 34 departments is causing a mental health crisis among secretariat employees. They need help — in their own language.

### Our Solution

The AP Sachivalayam AI Copilot is a Telugu-first AI assistant delivered over WhatsApp. It does not require employees to download a new app, learn a new interface, or type in English. They send a voice note in Telugu and get an answer in Telugu.

**10 integrated features:**

1. **Scheme Eligibility Checker** — RAG pipeline over 33 schemes. Employee describes a citizen's situation → AI checks eligibility across all schemes and lists required documents.

2. **Form Auto-Fill** — Employee dictates or types case details → AI extracts fields and generates government-format PDFs, submitted directly to the GSWS portal.

3. **Grievance Tracker** — AI routes complaints to the correct department, enforces SLA deadlines (24-120 hours), and auto-escalates breaches through 4 levels up to the State.

4. **Task Prioritizer** — AI generates a daily plan capped at 8 hours, sent via WhatsApp at 6 AM. Reduces decision overload and prevents burnout.

5. **Voice Input** — Whisper large-v3 transcribes Telugu voice notes and extracts structured data (names, ages, income, ration card type, scheme references).

6. **Outreach Scanner** — Weekly scan identifies citizens eligible for schemes they haven't applied for. No citizen left behind.

7. **Training Quizzes** — AI-generated scenarios help new employees learn 33 schemes through practice, not memorization.

8. **Document Checklist** — Given a citizen's profile, generates a consolidated document list across all eligible schemes. One visit, all documents.

9. **GO/Circular Knowledge Base** — Employees search government orders by asking "What changed in Amma Vodi eligibility?" and get answers citing specific GO numbers.

10. **Citizen Reminders** — Auto-scheduled WhatsApp reminders for pending documents, renewal deadlines, and payment disbursements. Citizens never miss a deadline.

### Technical Architecture

- **Backend:** FastAPI with 73 REST endpoints, async/await throughout
- **AI:** Claude (Anthropic) with circuit breaker for fault tolerance, PII stripping before every LLM call
- **Search:** 4-level RAG pipeline — Redis cache (0ms) → FAQ match (5ms) → pgvector semantic search (500ms) → keyword fallback (2s)
- **Voice:** Whisper large-v3 with Telugu domain vocabulary, FFmpeg audio processing
- **Database:** PostgreSQL 16 + pgvector for embeddings, Redis for caching
- **Workers:** Celery with 8 scheduled tasks (grievance SLA checks, daily plans, citizen reminders, outreach scans, GSWS sync)
- **Security:** Aadhaar bcrypt hashing (never stored raw), PII stripping, HMAC-SHA256 webhook verification, 5-level RBAC, full audit trails
- **Offline:** FAQ cache + keyword search + rule-based fallback work without internet
- **Testing:** 924 tests using real AP government data — not synthetic data, real scheme criteria from official portals
