## Key Materials to Prepare

For most applications, you'll need:

1. **Product demo video** (2-3 minutes) — show WhatsApp Telugu voice → scheme answer → form PDF flow
2. **Impact metrics** — hours saved, cost savings projections, beneficiary reach
3. **Pitch deck** (10-15 slides) — problem, solution, market size (1.3L employees), demo, team, ask
4. **One-pager** — for quick sharing with government officials and partners
5. **GitHub README** — clean, professional, with architecture diagram
6. **Live demo environment** — working WhatsApp bot for judges to test
